PhoenixRPU change log (v41, 2026-11-18 Pacific)

VERSION HISTORY
v0  Your uploaded sketch (backbox status lamps, attract light show). Flippers never turned on.
v1  Flipper fix: RPU_SetDisableFlippers(false) at the start of each ball; flippers off at game over.
v2  Original Phoenix sounds: PlayScoreSounds() pulses the 10/100/1,000/10,000 sound coils (solenoids 9-12)
    once per unit scored whenever a player's score goes up. Strength 2, 90 ms apart, max 5 pulses per sound.
v3  Drop-target reset pulse DROP_TARGET_RESET_STRENGTH 10 -> 30 (ms).
v4  Free play forced on at every power-up; MaximumCredits 40 -> 8; during ball 1 each Start press adds a player
    (up to 4), further presses add a credit (up to 8); AddCredit() always shows the credit count.
v5  Switch/sound audit against the manual's switch matrix (Figure 9). Every scoring switch already played its
    stock sound through the score coils, except the end-of-ball bonus countdown: it added 1,000 per step with
    no original sound. Now each bonus step pulses the 1,000 sound. New scenario 07-bonus-sounds.sim.
v6  Drop targets were pushed up but not latching. Reset pulse DROP_TARGET_RESET_STRENGTH 30 -> 50 ms, and the
    right bank now resets 300 ms after the left (DROP_BANK_RESET_STAGGER_MS) so the four big coils don't all fire
    together and starve each other of current. Scenario 08 (bonus countdown timing, worst case under 30 s) added in
    the test suite; no sketch change for it.
v7  Root cause for the weak drop-target reset: on the System Alpha (rev 200) the RPU library forces every coil push
    to a single pulse and ignores the "strength" number, so the 10 -> 30 -> 50 changes in v3/v6 had no effect.
    The pulse length is set only by RPU_SetSolenoidDefaultPulse(). The sketch never called it. Now called once
    after RPU_InitializeMPU() for all six drop-target reset coils (SOL_DROP_L_TOP/BOTTOM, R_TOP/BOTTOM,
    CENTER_LEFT/RIGHT) with DROP_TARGET_RESET_PULSE_MS = 60. The 300 ms bank stagger from v6 is kept.
    (v7 confirmed working on the machine: drop targets reset and latch; coils hit slightly harder than stock.)
v8  Game over tune on the original sound board: when the last ball ends, the four chimes play as a descending run
    (10,000 / 1,000 / 100 / 10) and the 10,000 chime once more, 220 ms apart (PlayGameOverTune(),
    GAME_OVER_TUNE_NOTE_MS, GAME_OVER_TUNE_ENABLED). New scenario 09-game-over-tune.sim.
    Drop reset pulse unchanged at DROP_TARGET_RESET_PULSE_MS = 60.
v9  Scoring matched to readings from the stock game: single drop target 100 -> 1,000 (SCORE_DROP_TARGET);
    completing a bank 1,100 -> 10,000 in all (SCORE_DROP_BANK_COMPLETE 1,000 -> 9,000, on top of the 1,000 for the
    last target). Center drop targets already matched (10,000). New scenario 10-drop-scores.sim.
v10 Bullseye scoring checked against stock readings: PH lit = 10,000 and PHO lit = 20,000 already matched the
    letter table (P/H 10,000, O/E 20,000, N/I 30,000, X 40,000, as printed on the playfield). Stock gave 60,000 with
    P-H-O-E lit and the extra ball lit, so collecting the lit extra ball at the bullseye now also scores
    SCORE_BULLSEYE_EXTRA_BALL = 40,000 on top of the letter value (20,000 + 40,000 = 60,000). Only when the extra
    ball is actually awarded. Inferred from that one reading. New scenario 11-bullseye-scores.sim.
v11 Repeatable PHOENIX. With P-H-O-E-N-I-X all lit, completing 1-5 again turns every letter off (and X and the outside
    specials) and starts a new round (the first set of a round spots P and H together, like the start of a game).
    Each completion is counted per player (PhoenixCompletions). From the moment of completion, and every 30 seconds
    after (PHOENIX_BLINK_INTERVAL_MS), all seven lamps blink once per completion (on 250 ms, off 250 ms,
    PHOENIX_BLINK_PERIOD_MS), then the lamps go back to showing progress. Two completions = two blinks per interval.
    The count resets each new game. A lit Extra Ball When Lit is kept through a restart. New scenarios
    12-phoenix-complete.sim and 13-phoenix-complete-twice.sim.
v12 Fewer extra balls. (1) The limit is now 5 extra balls per player per GAME in total (new ExtraBallsAwarded counter,
    reset each game); before, it only limited how many were held at once, so playing one off let you earn another.
    (2) The outside rollover special (after I) is a one-shot: collecting it turns it off and the next completion of 1-5
    lights it again; before, it paid out on every hit of the lit lane. (3) EXTRA BALL WHEN LIT is lit only when E is
    spotted (so once per PHOENIX round); before, it relit after every completion of 1-5 from E on.
    Extra ball sources now: bullseye with the lamp lit, bullseye with X lit (special), outside rollover special.
    Score awards (330,000 / 500,000) and match are credits, not extra balls. Scenario 03-extra-ball-cap.sim rewritten.
v13 One letter per completion of 1-5. The first completion used to spot P and H together; now it spots only P, and
    every completion after that spots exactly one letter: P, H, O, E, N, I, then X (seven completions), and the eighth
    completes PHOENIX and starts over (v11). Extra Ball When Lit now lights on the fourth completion (E) instead of the
    third. Scenarios 02, 03, 11, 12 and 13 updated for the new count. Note: the playfield's kickers and side targets
    also light numbers ("LITES 2/3/4"), so a set of 1-5 can be completed without hitting every rollover.
v14 Build check. Reported: P and H lit together on the first completion of 1-5, which v13 cannot do (the simulator

    The sketch version (14) now shows on the credits display for the first 8 seconds after power-up

    New scenario 14-version-display.sim.
v15 Between games (attract / game over) score displays 2-4 stay blank; only display 1 is used (power-up game version
    2026, then the high score and the last game's player 1 score, alternating). Displays 2-4 light up again as players

    longer writes display 1-4 all at once (Display_UpdateDisplays(0xFF) became display 0 only) and blanks displays 2-4
    each pass. New scenario 15-blank-displays-between-games.sim (two-player game played out, then displays checked
    during the match and in attract).
v16 Scores over 1,000,000: instead of blinking, the whole total (millions + six digits) scrolls in from the right
    across the display, rests on the normal score for 10 seconds, then scrolls again. Works in play and for the last
    game's score on display 1 in attract. Between games the attract light show now starts after 30 seconds of
    no switch activity (was 60), then follows the normal pattern (60 s show, 3 min dark, repeat, dark after 30 min).
    Build number 16 (shown in credits at power-up).
v17 Nothing scores, and the ball does not count as played, until it has been plunged and reaches one of the
    1-5 rollovers. A ball that drains before that is simply served again. Stops phantom points (about 1,400)
    when the ball is fed to the shooter lane and the drop banks reset. Header comment now states the version
    and what is new. Build number 17.
v18 Drop banks (4 targets each side) now always stand back up once all four are down. Cause: v17's "nothing scores
    before the first 1-5 rollover" rule returned early for EVERY switch, including drop targets, so a target that
    went down before the ball reached a rollover (the right bank sits next to the shooter lane) was never counted;
    the bank then never saw its 4th target and stayed down. Fixes: (1) drop-target hits made before the first
    rollover are counted quietly (no points, no bonus, ball still unplayed) and a bank completed that way resets;
    (2) the reset after the 4th target is now 100 ms (DROP_BANK_COMPLETE_RESET_DELAY_MS, was 500 ms);
    (3) backstop CheckDropBankBackstop(): if all four switches of a bank stay closed for 250 ms and no reset was
    asked for in the last 2.5 s, the bank is reset (no points), at most 3 times per bank per ball so a stuck
    switch cannot fire the coils forever. Also: the serial monitor (115200) now prints every number lamp change
    ("Number 2 lit, lamps now 02"), "1-5 complete: number lamps cleared", the letter count after each spot, and
    "PHOENIX complete: all letters off" -- added to chase the report of lamp 2 going out with no letter; the
    simulator could not reproduce that one, so the log will show what the code did. Build number 18.
    New scenarios 20-bank-after-rollover, 21-bank-hits-before-rollover (failed on v17, passes now),
    22-gate-still-holds, 23-bank-stuck-down.
v19 The bottom inlanes (left and right INSIDE rollovers) now light the "2" and "4" number lamps, exactly as the
    top "2" and "4" rollovers do. Before, they scored 1,000, advanced the bonus and reset the centre drop target
    but never lit a number, so a hit looked like it had not counted and the top rollover then scored the number
    later (reported on v16 and v18). Per the handbook, the left and right inside rollovers pair with the left and
    right centre drop targets, as "2" and "4" do. The outside rollovers are unchanged. Build number 19.
    New scenario 36-inside-rollovers-light-2-4 (fails on v18, passes now). Rules otherwise identical to v18.
v20 The "nothing scores until the first 1-5 rollover" rule (v17) now applies to BALL 1 ONLY, and on ball 1 the
    spinner also counts as a first hit, because a first ball can miss the top rollovers and go down the ramp to the
    spinner. From ball 2 on (any player) every switch scores immediately. Trade-off: on balls 2 and up the ball is
    "in play" from its first switch of any kind, so a ball that drains after only switch noise during the feed is no
    longer served again, and the early phantom points seen on ball 1 before v17 can come back on later balls.
    Build number 20. New scenarios 24-ball1-spinner-opens and 25-ball2-scores-immediately (both fail on v19);
    22-gate-still-holds now checks drop targets and a jet only (the spinner no longer belongs in it).
v21 Drop banks never stood back up mid-ball from ball 2 on (they stayed down until the next ball started). Cause:
    CountdownBonus() switches the solenoid stack off at the end of every ball, and only InitGamePlay() (start of a
    GAME) switched it back on. A solenoid push made without the "override" flag is silently dropped while the stack is
    off, and the bank resets (RequestDropBankReset, and the v18 backstop) are made without it. The resets at the
    start of each ball and the ball serve use the override flag, which is why those always worked. Fix: InitNewBall()
    now calls RPU_EnableSolenoidStack(). This bug is older than v17 and also hit v16 and earlier on balls 2+.
    New scenario 26-bank-resets-ball2 (fails on v20, passes now). Build number 21.
v22 Startup sound at the beginning of every game, on the original sound board only. When the Start button begins a game,
    InitGamePlay() calls the new PlayStartupTune(): a rising run of the four chimes (10 / 100 / 1,000 / 10,000, 150 ms
    apart, first note 100 ms after Start) and the 10,000 chime twice more after a short pause (about 1 s in all). It is the
    mirror image of the v8 game over tune and uses the same sound coils and pulse strength. STARTUP_TUNE_ENABLED,
    STARTUP_TUNE_NOTE_MS and STARTUP_TUNE_START_DELAY_MS tune it. It plays once per game (not when a player is added
    on ball 1). Not tested in the simulator (rpu-sim is macOS only); needs a listen on the machine. Build number 22.
v23 COMBO CODES + RACE THE CLOCK (RULES_COMBO_CODES and RULES_RACE_CLOCK, both true; each can be used alone, both false = classic v21 rules).
    Combo codes: a letter is earned by hitting the five top 1-5 rollovers in that letter's code, every code using each shot once.
      P 1-2-3-4-5, H 5-4-3-2-1, O/E/N/I/X and the restart step (after X) get a random order, picked once per player per game
      (reseeded each game; losing and re-earning a letter gives the same code). Progress starts over each ball and after any wrong
      shot. The code shows in the left five digits of the score display: at ball start (until the ball is plunged plus 3 s), after
      a wrong shot, and when a letter becomes current, for 3 s. P, H, O and E show it every time; N, I, X and the restart code only
      once, when they become current (COMBO_HIDDEN_FROM_STEP). P, H, O blink the next shot as a hint (COMBO_HINT_THROUGH_STEP).
      Hit shots light steady; a wrong shot flashes the lit lamps fast for 1 s; a completed code flashes all five 3 times. The
      current letter slow-blinks. The same lane twice within 400 ms counts once. ONLY the five top rollovers are shots: kickers,
      side targets and inside rollovers no longer light 1-5 (they still score and the inside rollovers still reset the centre
      drop targets). With the clock off, I has a 30 s and X a 20 s timer from their first shot.
    Race the clock: the letter being worked on has a countdown, P 2:30, H 2:15, O 2:00, E 1:45, N 1:30, I 1:15, X 1:00 (the
      restart step after X also 1:00); earning a letter or losing one starts that letter's full time. The clock starts at the first
      top 1-5 rollover of a ball and pauses at drain or ball save until the next ball's first rollover; time carries over. Status
      display: seconds in the credit digits (last 10 s blink), over 99 s minutes in the credit digits and seconds in the ball digits
      (ball number hidden). Timeout loses one letter (X first, then I, N, ...) with three falling chimes; E lost turns the lit
      extra ball off, I lost turns the outside specials off. Timeout on P ends that player's game only: flippers go off, the ball
      drains, the bonus counts, and that player is skipped from then on; the game ends when everyone is out.
      Spinner +2 s per spin, up to 20 s per ball. Both drop banks completed once lights the bullseye for time: lamp 16 blinks
      (time only) or is solid (extra ball, alone or with time); a bullseye hit adds 15 s, and both banks must be completed again.
      The extra ball from E can be collected once per game per player (losing and re-earning E does not relight it).
      Maximum clock 99:59. All numbers are constants at the top of the new code block, to be tuned in play.
    Not run on the machine or in rpu-sim (macOS only). Logic checked with a host-side test harness (combo sequences, bounce filter,
    random codes are permutations, clock countdown, timeouts and elimination, pause rules, spinner cap, bullseye lighting, lamp 16,
    once-per-game E extra ball, status display digits, classic mode unchanged) and the whole sketch passes a syntax check against
    stub Arduino headers; it has not been compiled with the AVR toolchain. Build number 23.

v28 COMBO CODES ARE A CHECKLIST (ANY ORDER). The fixed order of v27 is gone. Each letter now has a checklist of targets; hit them all, in any
    order, to earn the letter. Nothing resets on a wrong shot - targets that are not on the list (or already ticked) are simply ignored.
      Items: the rollovers 1-5 (ONE item: all five lit, any order; "1" or "5" lights both like the classic rules), LB (any target of the left
        bank), RB (right bank), SP (spinner, ticks on its first spin), BE (bullseye).
      Checklist size: P 1 item (the rollovers), H 2 (rollovers + one more), O 3, E 3, N 4, I 4, X 5, then the restart step 3. Items are picked
        at random once per player per game (P and H always include the rollovers); the order on the screen is always 1-5, LB, RB, SP, BE.
      Race the clock starts with the first valid target of the checklist. Without the clock, I and X still have their time limit from the first
        valid target (30 s / 20 s); too slow = the checklist starts over ("TOO SLOW - START OVER"). A new ball also starts the checklist over.
      Nextion: the boxes show the letter's items (1-5, LB, RB, SP, BE), unused boxes are blank. To do = orange, done = green, rollovers in
        progress show 1/5 ... 4/5 in yellow. The callout names each tick ("SPINNER DONE", "ROLLOVERS 1-5 DONE"), then "CHECKLIST COMPLETE";
        the rest of the time it lists what is left ("TO DO: LB RB SP BE"). The checklist is always on the screen (no hiding of N, I, X).
      Score display: digits of the items still to do, left aligned, for 3 s (and until the ball is plunged): 1 = rollovers, 2 = LB, 3 = RB,
        4 = SP, 5 = BE. Rollover lamps show the rollover progress when the rollovers are on the list, and flash when the checklist is complete.
      Removed: wrong-shot reset and flash, bounce windows (ComboIgnoreMs), hint blink, hide-after-3-seconds (COMBO_HIDDEN_FROM_STEP,
        NX_ALWAYS_SHOW_CODE), COMBO_WRONG_FLASH_MS. Rules texts of rulesets 2 and 4 reworded.
    Host tests: ALL TESTS PASSED (checklist generation, any-order ticking, ignored targets, rollover progress, I timer, display, lamps, screen
